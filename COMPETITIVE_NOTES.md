# Competitive Notes — how tshirtdeli wins

Denise's market intel, captured 2026-08-14. CC + Claude: read this when making
pricing, UX, or messaging decisions — it's the "why" behind several features.

## Our edge: SPEED (and pickup)

- We turn orders around much faster than the big competitors (small-shop
  advantage). They start their delivery pricing at ~2 weeks out: "Order today,
  free delivery Fri Aug 21, Rush Delivery Aug 12" (rush costs extra).
- Their 1-item price is a little lower than ours — but the customer waits far
  longer for that price, and they offer NO pickup. We do.
- Strategic read: their model punishes urgency; ours rewards it. Our story is
  "same shirt, days sooner, pickup if you want it" — worth making loud in
  site messaging, product pages, and checkout.
- Future feature idea (unscoped): show a concrete promise on our site —
  estimated ready/pickup date at design or checkout time ("ready by X").
  Weaponizes the turnaround edge the same way competitors weaponize price.

## Competitor pricing calculators (future consideration)

- Competitors show a pricing calculator with a slider: incremental discounts
  starting as low as 2 tees (1%), ticking up per quantity.
- Note for feasibility: our volume-tier system already stores an arbitrary
  ladder per garment (any number of rows), so a slider/calculator is mostly a
  DISPLAY feature on top of existing data — not a new pricing engine.
- Deliberately NOT adopted yet: our chunky tiers (e.g. 6/12/24) may be smarter
  for a DTG/embroidery shop (costs don't amortize like screen print). Micro-
  discounts from 2+ shave margin on small orders. Revisit with real order data.

## Standing posture

Be a smart competitor: compete on speed, pickup, and design-tool experience —
not on being the cheapest single shirt.
